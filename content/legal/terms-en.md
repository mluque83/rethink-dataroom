# Data room terms of use

1. **Personal access.** Access is granted to the holder of the invited e-mail address. Verifying a code sent to that address proves control of the mailbox, not physical identity, and does not technically prevent credential sharing; sharing is prohibited.
2. **Scope.** You can only see information Rethink Medical has enabled for you. The absence of a document implies neither that it exists nor that it does not.
3. **Watermarks.** Documents display a personalised watermark (name, verified e-mail, access identifier, date) to deter disclosure and support attribution. Do not remove or hide it.
4. **Downloads.** Available only where expressly enabled; by default personalised watermarked copies. Downloaded copies remain subject to the NDA.
5. **Activity logging.** Access, documents and versions opened, pages displayed, downloads and an estimate of active time are logged as described in the privacy notice. Opening a document does not imply having read it.
6. **Revocation.** Rethink Medical may change, suspend or revoke access at any time. Revocation does not erase information already seen or copies already downloaded, which remain subject to the NDA.
7. **Questions.** If enabled, your questions are private between you and Rethink Medical unless you are told otherwise.
8. **Forward-looking information.** Forecasts, plans and estimates are uncertain. Documents show their date and status; unverified data is labelled as such.
