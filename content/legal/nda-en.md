# Non-disclosure agreement (NDA) — Rethink Medical S.L. data room

## 1. Parties
1.1. **Rethink Medical S.L.** ("Rethink Medical" or the "Disclosing Party"), registered office and tax ID **[TO BE CONFIRMED]**.
1.2. The person accepting this agreement in the data room (the "Recipient"), identified by their verified e-mail address and by the identity declared upon acceptance.
1.3. **Capacity.** If the Recipient states that they act on behalf of an entity, they declare under their own responsibility that they have sufficient authority to bind it. Rethink Medical does not presume such authority merely because a company name is entered and may request evidence of it. Otherwise the Recipient accepts in a personal capacity.

## 2. Permitted purpose
The Recipient may use Confidential Information solely to evaluate a potential investment, financing, collaboration or strategic relationship with Rethink Medical (the "Purpose"). Any other use is prohibited.

## 3. Confidential Information
3.1. Any information, in any form, accessed through or in connection with the data room, including without limitation corporate, financial, tax, commercial, technical, regulatory, clinical, quality, intellectual-property, manufacturing and personnel information; documents, data, analyses, forecasts, questions and answers; and the existence and content of discussions about a potential transaction.
3.2. Forecasts, plans and estimates are forward-looking, uncertain and not a guarantee of results.

## 4. Exclusions
Information the Recipient can demonstrate by documentary evidence: (a) was public when disclosed or later became public through no breach by the Recipient; (b) was lawfully in its possession without confidentiality obligations before disclosure; (c) was lawfully received from a third party not bound by confidentiality; or (d) was independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

## 5. Recipient obligations
5.1. Keep Confidential Information strictly confidential and protect it with at least reasonable care.
5.2. Not disclose, publish, distribute, assign, sell or make it available to third parties without Rethink Medical's prior written authorisation.
5.3. **Personal credentials.** Not share access links, verification codes, sessions or other data-room credentials. Access is personal and non-transferable.
5.4. **Reproductions.** No screenshots, photographs, screen or video recordings, transcriptions or other reproductions without Rethink Medical's written authorisation, except downloads expressly enabled in the data room, which remain subject to this agreement.
5.5. **External tools and AI.** Not upload, copy or input Confidential Information into artificial-intelligence tools, translation, storage or processing services or any third-party service without Rethink Medical's prior written authorisation.
5.6. **Advisers.** The Recipient's advisers may access Confidential Information only if authorised by Rethink Medical and through their own individual data-room access, accepting their own terms.

## 6. Legally required disclosure
If legally required to disclose, the Recipient shall notify Rethink Medical as early as legally permitted, reasonably cooperate so that protective measures can be sought, and disclose only what is strictly required.

## 7. Intellectual property
No licence or assignment of any intellectual or industrial property right, patent, trade mark, trade secret or other right is granted.

## 8. No obligation
Neither data-room access nor this agreement obliges any party to negotiate or close any investment or transaction. No representation or warranty is given through the data room except as may be agreed in a definitive contract.
